Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Finn, a mobile application provided by Happy Deswal, handles information when you use its finance-management, automatic tracking, AI and advertising features.

1. Information We Process

  • Profile information: Your name, country, occupation and currency provided during onboarding.
  • Financial information: Transaction amounts, titles or merchant names, categories, dates, notes and budget limits you enter, or that are created through optional automatic tracking.
  • Chat information: Messages you send to Finn AI, responses and financial summaries used to generate insights.
  • App-generated identifier: Finn creates an identifier to associate backend registration and daily AI usage limits with an app installation. This identifier may be linked to your profile.
  • Network, advertising and diagnostic information: The backend and service providers may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app and advertising interactions, and diagnostic information to operate services, display or measure advertisements and help prevent misuse.

2. Optional SMS-Based Money Management

With your explicit consent and Android SMS permission, Finn processes incoming bank debit and credit SMS alerts on your device, including while the app is in the background, to automatically record and categorize transactions and update your totals and budgets. You do not need to open or mark a bank message as read for an eligible incoming alert to be processed.

Finn requests RECEIVE_SMS for this feature. It does not request permission to send SMS, read existing inbox history or access call logs. Only new alerts received after you enable tracking and grant the required permission are eligible for automatic import.

Incoming message text is parsed locally. The parser filters out OTPs, failed or pending payments, and messages that are not recognized as completed financial transaction alerts. Finn does not store or upload the full SMS text through its automatic tracking feature.

Finn saves extracted transaction details and hashed message or reference fingerprints to help prevent duplicate entries. These fingerprints are used for duplicate detection, not advertising. Saved transaction details may subsequently be included in financial summaries used by Finn's AI features, as explained below.

SMS tracking is optional. You can continue adding transactions manually, disable tracking in Quick Actions & Services → Auto tracking, or revoke SMS permission in Android settings. Disabling access stops new SMS imports but does not delete previously saved transactions.

Automatically created entries may need correction and can be edited or deleted. Finn does not access your bank account directly or request your bank login credentials.

Only recognized alerts in the currency selected in your Finn profile are imported. Bank and payment-message formats vary; some alerts may not be recognized or may be assigned an incorrect category. Review imported entries and correct them or add a transaction manually when needed. Finn balances reflect saved entries, not a live bank balance.

3. Optional Notification Access

If you separately enable payment-notification tracking and grant Android notification access, Finn can process newly posted notifications from supported payment and messaging apps, including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and supported SMS apps.

Finn checks the source app before reading notification text and ignores unsupported apps. It does not scan existing notification history.

Recognized financial alerts are processed on your device in the same way as SMS alerts. Full notification text is not stored or uploaded by this tracking feature. Extracted transaction details and duplicate-detection fingerprints may be saved locally.

You can disable this feature in Auto tracking or revoke notification access in Android settings.

4. How Information Is Used and Shared

  • Finance tracking: Locally saved transactions and budgets are used to calculate balances, category totals, reports and budgeting information.
  • Backend registration: Your profile details and app-generated identifier are sent to Finn's backend to register the installation and support app services. The backend can retain registration records and, when configured, send registration details or user summaries to the developer through email.
  • AI features: Finn can send chat messages, your name, country and currency information, app-generated identifier and financial summaries to its backend. Financial summaries may include information derived from automatically tracked transactions. The backend can forward your prompt, financial context and name to configured AI providers, such as Groq or Google Gemini, to generate responses, reports or financial insights. Some app insights can request these summaries automatically. Raw SMS and payment-notification bodies are not included by the tracking importer.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob and related Google services process information to deliver and measure advertising, diagnose service issues and help prevent fraud. Finn does not use SMS or payment-notification text to target advertising.

AI processing is not entirely on-device. Information sent to external providers is handled under the applicable service terms, settings and data-processing arrangements.

Avoid entering bank passwords, PINs, OTPs or other unnecessary sensitive information into chat messages or transaction notes.

5. Third-Party Services

The services used depend on the feature and backend configuration. Relevant provider privacy information and terms include:

  • Google Privacy Policy — Google Play Services, Google advertising services and developer email services.
  • Google AdMob privacy information .
  • Render Privacy Policy — backend hosting.
  • Groq Privacy Policy and GroqCloud Data Processing Addendum — configured AI processing.
  • Google Gemini API terms — configured AI processing.

6. Data Storage and Security

Transactions, budgets, chat history and tracking fingerprints are stored primarily in Finn's app-private storage on your device. The transaction database does not currently use app-level database encryption. Android's storage protections depend on your device and its security settings.

Finn's configured backend connections use HTTPS. Registration records are stored by the backend, and registration emails, service logs and information sent to providers can exist outside your device. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices, Retention and Deletion

Local app records remain until you remove them using available app controls, clear Finn's app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. You can edit transactions and delete automatically tracked entries in Finn. Duplicate-detection fingerprints can remain in local app storage after an individual transaction is deleted. Clear Finn's app storage or uninstall the app to remove these local fingerprints.

Exported reports or files saved outside the app must be removed separately. Clearing app storage or uninstalling does not delete backend records, developer emails or data already handled by providers.

The current backend does not automatically expire registration records. They may remain until removed by the developer or until backend storage is reset. Provider-held records, logs and emails may be retained according to their applicable terms and settings. Finn does not currently provide an automatic in-app backend deletion function.

You can use the contact options on happydeswal.in to request access, correction or deletion of developer-held information. Include enough information to identify your Finn profile and describe your request. Do not send passwords, PINs, OTPs or full bank messages.

You can independently revoke SMS permission and notification access through Android settings. Turning off tracking does not automatically erase earlier entries or information already shared with backend services.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when Finn's features or information-handling practices change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Where additional consent is required for a new use of information, that consent must be obtained before the new use begins.
